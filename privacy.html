Privacy Policy

Saleh Personal AI Assistant is a personal application used only by its owner.

  • It accesses Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts and Google Drive only to serve its owner, at the owner's request.
  • It does not sell any data.
  • It does not share any data with third parties.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer.
  • Write actions (sending an email, creating a calendar event) happen only after explicit confirmation by the owner.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account: myaccount.google.com/permissions.
